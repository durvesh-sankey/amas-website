Why factories struggle today

Disconnected systems. No intelligence.

Factories run on a patchwork of platforms. Each one stores critical information, but none can connect the dots. By the time data is collected, analysed by hand and acted on, the moment to act has often passed.

Margin squeezeVolatile material and energy costs leave little room for inefficiency.
Rising compliance loadDocumentation and audit preparation take days of manual effort across systems.
Slow root causeProduction, lab, process and quality data sit in silos, so tracing one failure takes days.
Unplanned downtimeWhen a unit stops, the cause is unclear and the wrong fix gets applied.

Every system stores information. None provide intelligence.
